Average decoding accuracy and peak-normalized alignment trajectories across relative network depth. Each trajectory is interpolated onto 101 evenly spaced positions (0 = first hidden-state layer, 1 = last); shaded bands show the 95% CI of the across-model mean. Left: instruction-tuned models without instructions (green, n=28), instruction-tuned models with instructions (pink dashed, n=28), and base/pretrained models (orange, n=14). The dashed gray line marks the average permutation-based 95th-percentile significance threshold from the instruction-tuned models without instructions. Right: instruction-tuned models with instructions (green, n=28) and without instructions (pink dashed, n=28). Each model's alignment coefficients are divided by their own peak within each condition before interpolation and averaging. Base models contribute only to decoding. Missing instruction-conditioned results are omitted from their respective averages.
