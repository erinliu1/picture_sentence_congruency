Average decoding accuracy trajectory and average alignment coefficient trajectory relative to model peak, across normalized network depth (28 models). Left: the layerwise congruent-vs-incongruent decoding accuracy of each model was interpolated onto 101 evenly spaced normalized depth positions (0 = first hidden-state layer, 1 = last), then averaged across all 28 models; the shaded band is the 95% CI of the across-model mean at each depth. The dotted gray line marks the average permutation-based 95th-percentile significance threshold (from layerwise_significance.csv), averaged across all 28 models. Right: each model's layerwise alignment coefficient (the OLS regression coefficient of human congruency ratings on the model's decoded congruent-probability) was first divided by that model's own peak coefficient, so every model's trajectory has a peak of 1 regardless of its absolute alignment strength; these peak-normalized trajectories were then interpolated onto the same 101-point normalized-depth grid and averaged across all 28 models, with the shaded band showing the 95% CI of the across-model mean. Together the two panels show the common layerwise shape of decodability and human-alignment across architecturally and scale-diverse vision-language models.
