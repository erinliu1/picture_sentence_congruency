Each panel plots one model's decoded congruency probability (z-scored across items, then centered within its 4-item stimulus set) against its centered behavioral congruency rating, at the model's most human-aligned layer, colored by ground-truth condition (green: congruent, red: incongruent). The dashed line is the OLS fit of rating on probability; its slope (beta) matches that layer's coefficient in coefficients.csv. Gemma4-31B: beta=1.881, LLaVA-OV-7B: beta=0.556. Gemma4-31B's decoded representations track behavioral congruency judgments more closely than LLaVA-OV-7B's (larger beta).
