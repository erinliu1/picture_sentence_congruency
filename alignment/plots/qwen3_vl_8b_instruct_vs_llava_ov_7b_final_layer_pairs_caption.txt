Each panel plots one model's decoded congruency probability (z-scored across items, then centered within its 4-item stimulus set) against its centered behavioral congruency rating, at the model's final layer, colored by ground-truth condition (green: congruent, red: incongruent). The dashed line is the OLS fit of rating on probability; its slope (beta) matches that layer's coefficient in coefficients.csv. Qwen3-VL-8B: beta=1.574, LLaVA-OV-7B: beta=0.544. Qwen3-VL-8B's decoded representations track behavioral congruency judgments more closely than LLaVA-OV-7B's (larger beta).
