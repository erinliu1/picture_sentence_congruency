Behavioral performance as a function of model size. Panels show classification accuracy (left), within-item concordance (center), and Spearman rank correlation with mean human ratings (right) for 28 instruction-tuned vision-language models across seven families. Each model is evaluated on 320 picture-sentence pairs from 80 stimulus sets; individual model markers are omitted. Accuracy classifies a pair as congruent when its expected 1-5 rating is greater than 3 and incongruent otherwise. Concordance is the proportion of the four within-item comparisons in which a congruent pair receives a strictly higher rating than an incongruent pair, averaged over items; ties do not count as concordant. Spearman's rho compares model and human ratings across matched picture-sentence pairs. Colors identify model families. The horizontal axis is logarithmic and uses the leading numeric value of the registered model-size label in billions (total rather than active parameters for MoE models). Curves use shape-preserving PCHIP interpolation in log-size space for families with at least three models; two-model families are joined by straight segments. Curves are visual guides through measured model values, not fitted scaling laws. No uncertainty intervals are shown.
